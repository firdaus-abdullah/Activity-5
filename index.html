🎤 Debrief — Present Your Test Matrix

Each group has 2 minutes to present their test matrix and calculation answers. Structure your presentation as follows:

  • Show your test matrix — for each product, which QC tests did you select and why?
  • Explain why sterile products do not need USP <61>/<62> and why non-sterile products do not need USP <71>.
  • Walk through your calculation answers — show the working.
  • Justify your reference citations — which USP chapters apply to which product.
  • Be ready to defend your rationale — other groups can challenge your decisions.
